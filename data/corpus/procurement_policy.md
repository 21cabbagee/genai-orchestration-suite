# Procurement and Vendor Policy

## Purchase requisitions
Any purchase above INR 50,000 needs a purchase requisition in the procurement system before a vendor is engaged. Purchases below INR 50,000 can use a corporate card with a receipt.

## Quotations
Purchases between INR 50,000 and INR 10,00,000 need at least two competing quotations. Purchases above INR 10,00,000 need three quotations and approval from the procurement committee.

## Vendor onboarding
New vendors must submit a GST certificate, PAN, cancelled cheque and signed code of conduct. Vendor master data is verified by finance before the first purchase order is issued. Onboarding normally takes 5 business days.

## Invoices and payment terms
Vendor invoices must quote a valid purchase order number. Standard payment terms are 45 days from invoice receipt. Invoices without a matching purchase order and goods receipt are put on hold.
