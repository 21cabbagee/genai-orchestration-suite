# IT Access and Device Policy

## Requesting system access
Access to business applications is requested through the IT service desk using the Access Request form. The request must name the application, the role needed and a business justification. The line manager approves the request before IT provisions it.

## Provisioning timelines
Standard access requests are fulfilled within 2 business days. Access to production databases or finance systems requires an additional security review and takes up to 5 business days.

## Password resets and lockouts
Locked accounts can be unlocked through the self-service password portal after multi-factor verification. If self-service fails, the service desk resets the password after verifying the employee's identity by video call.

## Laptops and hardware
New joiners receive a standard laptop on day one. Replacement laptops are issued when a device is older than 4 years or is damaged; damage must be reported within 48 hours. Lost devices must be reported to security immediately so the device can be remotely wiped.
