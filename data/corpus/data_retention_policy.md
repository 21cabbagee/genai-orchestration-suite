# Data Protection and Retention Policy

## Personal data handling
Customer personal data such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, PAN and card details must only be stored in approved systems. Personal data must never be pasted into public AI tools or shared over personal email.

## Retention periods
Customer support transcripts are retained for 2 years. Financial records, invoices and expense claims are retained for 8 years to meet statutory audit requirements. Recruitment data of unsuccessful candidates is deleted after 12 months.

## Data subject requests
Requests from customers to access or delete their data must be logged with the privacy team within 1 business day and completed within 30 days.

## Incident reporting
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the security team within 2 hours of discovery. The security team assesses whether regulators and affected customers must be notified.
