# Travel and Expense Policy

## Eligible expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for business travel, client meals, conference fees and approved software subscriptions. Personal entertainment, fines and alcohol without a client present are not reimbursable.

## Claim deadlines
Expense claims must be submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days are rejected unless the employee's director approves a late-submission exception.

## Receipts and limits
An itemised receipt is mandatory for every expense above INR 500. Hotel stays are capped at INR 9,000 per night in metro cities and INR 6,000 per night elsewhere. Client meals are capped at INR 2,500 per attendee.

## Approval and payment
Claims up to INR 25,000 are approved by the line manager. Claims above INR 25,000 also need finance controller approval. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll cycle, usually within 10 business days.
