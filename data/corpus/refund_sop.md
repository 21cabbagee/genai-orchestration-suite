# Customer Refund Standard Operating Procedure

## Refund eligibility
Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the subscription has not been used for more than 5 sessions. After 14 days, refunds are prorated for the unused months of an annual plan.

## Processing steps
The support agent verifies the order ID, checks usage in the billing console, and raises a refund ticket. Refunds up to INR 20,000 are approved by the support lead. Larger refunds need approval from the finance team.

## Timelines
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 business days. Card refunds may take an additional 3 to 5 days to appear on the customer's statement.

## Chargebacks and disputes
If a customer has raised a chargeback with their bank, the refund ticket must be paused and escalated to the payments risk team. Agents must not promise a refund while a dispute is open.
